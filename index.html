About

Developer by craft, founder by default.

I don't just ship code — I run the businesses that depend on it. Every store, starter and automation in this portfolio is something I built, launched and operate.

From Khulna, Bangladesh, I founded NodeX Station to build production-grade software for clients and for my own product line: 24 MERN starter products live on Gumroad, two running e-commerce brands, and automation systems that handle outreach, content and lead generation.

My focus sits where engineering meets revenue — checkout flows that convert, inventory that's accurate in real time, and automations that quietly do the repetitive work so a small team can move like a big one.
